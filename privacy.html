Claude 1 Privacy Policy

Claude 1 is used only by its owner to upload, schedule and manage videos on the owner's own YouTube channels through the YouTube Data API.

It accesses only the signed-in owner's YouTube account. The sign-in token is stored on the owner's own computer. No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Access can be removed at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
